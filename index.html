📌 Weekend Update

The countdown is on! As we approach this weekend, here are a few key details to help you prepare:

  1. Rain is on the horizon: Although it won't dampen our spirits, please keep an umbrella or waterproof layer handy.
  2. Footwear: Because it's an outdoor farm venue, the ground is uneven. High heels are not recommended! Feel free to wear trainers, sandals, or whatever keeps you comfortable.
  3. Seating: If we get sunshine, feel free to bring a picnic blanket or camping chairs—especially if you have little ones or prefer seating with back support, as main seating will be picnic-style benches.
  4. Children's Book Swap: Bringing little ones? Don't forget to bring a pre-loved book to swap! 📚
  5. Early Arrivals: For guests travelling from further afield, please let Fiona or Manny know if you expect to arrive before 1:30 PM so we can inform the venue.
  6. Taxis: We can't wait to celebrate, but please pre-book your taxis in advance! The venue is in a rural location, so pre-booking is essential.

Manny, Fi, Hattie and Bea can't wait to see you on Saturday! ❤️
